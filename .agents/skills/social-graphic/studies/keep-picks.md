# Study: the user's Keep picks (October 2026)

Source: the 28 posts the user kept from 21 accounts (links in
[picks-2026-10.md](./picks-2026-10.md)). Each kept post was looked at, and each
group was measured with the composition harness in `review/` terms (bare ground,
tonal levels, weight). Tags: M = measured, O = observed, I = inferred.

Almost every kept post is on a dark ground. Agenta posts are white or yellow
only, so every technique below is rebuilt for a light ground. We keep the
technique and drop the look.

## What each group does, and what we built from it

1. **Defocused field → `haze`.** The whole canvas is an out-of-focus light
   field with no edge in it (O), carrying only a centred partner lockup and a
   thin slash between the two names (O). Nothing is sharp except the type, so
   the type reads at a small cap (about 5 %H, I). On Agenta it becomes paper,
   white and yellow blurred into one field, with the ink lockup.
2. **Light bloom → `aurora`.** A band of saturated light rises from the bottom
   edge like a wave (O). The title sits centred above the crest, small, at about
   8–11 %H cap (I). On Agenta it becomes a yellow wave rising out of paper.
3. **Minimal pair → `connect`.** Two lockups on a faint grid, joined by one
   connector line (Mistral 5, O). The line says "integration" without a word.
   On Agenta: a paper blueprint grid, two white cards and an ink connector with
   end nodes.
4. **Light bloom with tiles → `spotlight`.** Three app icons in a row, the
   brand's raised in the middle, with a fan of light behind it (Lovable 3, O).
   On Agenta: the Agenta tile raised between two partner tiles, white beams on
   a yellow ground.
5. **Monumental number → `milestone`.** One number at 25–35 %H, its fill
   shading from bright to dim top to bottom, and a framed row of names beneath
   (Clerk 1, O). On Agenta: a GT Alpina Light numeral shading from ink to
   ink-3, a hairline frame of names as texture. Real numbers only.
6. **Product on light → `prompt`.** A model or feature name above a large
   prompt box, both floating on soft light (Bolt 6, O). On Agenta: the
   composer component, with the model chip and the primary send key, over a
   yellow bloom.
7. **Mono mark → `monogram`.** A large title on the left and the brand's mark
   at monumental size on the right, in one colour (v0 5, O). It is balanced,
   unlike a title-only layout. On Agenta: the black mark at about 62 %H, not
   recoloured and not stretched.
8. **Option tiles → `tiles`.** A centred row of four rounded tiles with line
   icons and labels, one flagged "New" (Bolt 4, O). On Agenta: four tiles, the
   new one carrying the single yellow badge.

## Not taken

- Dark grounds in every group, because Agenta posts are white or yellow only.
- Photographs and generated imagery (Cohere 5, Runway's footage), because the
  brand forbids stock and generated imagery.
- Saturated hues beyond the brand: Lovable's pink and blue, Modal's green,
  Runway's colour casts. Agenta light is yellow, paper, white and ink.
- Chrome or metallic type (Clerk 1). Only the top-to-bottom shading survives.

## Do not copy

- Lovable's pink-and-blue wave.
- Runway's blurred film stills.
- Modal's green glow.
- v0's asterisk mark.
- Clerk's chrome numerals.
- Bolt's product UI.

We took the technique and the measured composition, never the colour, the
mark or the screen.
